## 0. What this is

Once the night is in, and all through the chase, the woods have figures in them. A director on
the host runs episodes: for a dozen or twenty seconds, every few seconds, a shade stands up at
the edge of a player's sight, faces them, and is gone again when they come near, when they look
at it too long, or when its time is up. On every screen that has one near, a slight vignette
comes in and the whispers start. Most shades are nothing. One in an episode, sometimes, is a
lunge: it walks straight at its player, bending only a little toward them, and kills on contact;
a player who steps out of its line sees it pass and dissolve.

In the chase the shades also show the way. A shade stands beside the open way home, ahead of its
player, so the thing that hunts the party is what points them down the mountain.

Shades, lunges and the Hollows that step out at the forks all come in from nothing and go out to
nothing: the shadow's look. And the fork Hollows give more warning than they did.

## 1. The director (`sim/haunt.ts`)

Host-only, on `World.haunt`, off the wire and outside the fingerprint, with its own stream
(`worldSeed ^ HAUNT_SALT`).

**When.** The chase, or the climb once `actsUnder(bestClimb).night` is at least `HAUNT_NIGHT_MIN`
(0.3), where the best climb is the living player nearest the crest. By day there is nothing, and
no draw is spent.

**Episodes.** A rest of 40 to 85 s on the climb and 16 to 36 s in the chase, then an episode of
14 to 24 s with 3 to 6 shades at 2.5 to 5 s intervals, each for a drawn living player (in the chase
one not yet safe). With a chance of 0.35 on the climb and 0.5 in the chase the episode's last shade
is a lunge.

**Where a shade stands.** By the watcher's method in the shade's own band: a drawn side, 16° to
40° off the player's look, 9 to 22 m out, in the mist at the player's side (as first built, 12
to 28 m and 30° to 70° off, drawn as the unlit Hollow, they were never seen; see
[`2026-10-06-the-mist-shades.md`](2026-10-06-the-mist-shades.md) for the look); standable, off the road corridor, at least 6 m from every living player, in a clear
sightline from the player's eye. Six tries a tick. In the chase a harmless shade is first tried
beside the open way home: the nearest guide node within 15 to 60 m that is nearer the pad than the
player's own node, the shade 55 to 80 % of the way to it and 3 m to a drawn side, within the wide
view of the player's look; failing that, the band.

**What a shade does.** Faces its player for 6 to 12 s. It is gone the first tick any living player
is within `SHADE_FLEE_RADIUS` (5 m), or once a player has had it in the stare's 20° cone for
`SHADE_WATCHED_S` (2.5 s) in all, or when its time is up. It is not a Hollow: no contact, no stare,
no slowing.

**What a lunge does.** Starts 16 to 26 m out and walks at `LUNGE_SPEED` (6 m/s, under a sprint) along
a line through its player and 60 m past them, the line drifting toward where the player now is by
at most `LUNGE_DRIFT` (1.2 m/s). It is a Hollow for contact and for the stare (the dark closes as it
comes), never for the pack's retargeting. It is gone once the player is behind it, once it has
walked `LUNGE_MAX_S` (8 s), once stuck, or at the end of its line.

## 2. On the screen

- **The lens** (`game/escalation.ts`): `haunt` is 1 while any shade stands within `HAUNT_NEAR`
  (60 m) of the local eye, eased in over 2 s and out over 6 s; the lens's dread is lifted to
  `HAUNT_DREAD` (0.4) of it, a slight vignette under the stare's.
- **The whispers** (`game/stareAudio.ts`): the same voices as the stare's, at
  `HAUNT_WHISPER_SHARE` (0.6) of their level by the haunt alone, or the stare's if more.
- **The look** (`game/entityViews.ts`): a shade, a lunge or a Hollow stepping out at a fork
  comes in from nothing over `SHADE_FADE_IN_S` (0.7 s); a shade or a lunge gone from the state
  goes out over `SHADE_FADE_OUT_S` (0.9 s), its model held until then. Both are drawn as the
  Hollow.
- **The chase's cast** is stronger than it was: `CHASE_TINT` (1.2, 0.55, 0.7), the material
  path's shadows deepened by 50.

## 3. The forks

A fork is cut at `FORK_CUT_RADIUS` 14 m (was 9) and its Hollow stands `FORK_REVEAL_S` 2.5 s at the
mouth (was 1): a player walking through has it come in behind them with time to see it, where it
stood up in their face before.

## 4. Tests

`test/sim/haunt.test.ts`: the record and its day; haunting by the best living climb and in the
chase; a shade's place, facing and harmlessness; gone when neared, watched or timed out; a lunge's
walk, its kill on a player who stands, and its passing and going on one who steps aside; the chase's
placement beside the way home. `test/sim/cut.test.ts` re-pins the fork's timing. The Hollow tests
(`cut`, `summit`, `summitRun`, `hollowWalk`) turn the haunt off, since they pin the Hollows alone.
