# The stare

**Date:** 2026-10-05
**Status:** Built 2026-10-05.
**Parent:** [`2026-09-16-the-summit.md`](2026-09-16-the-summit.md) §2 and §4, whose stare filled in
six seconds, emptied in three and killed at 1, and showed as the whole frame dimming to black
under a round vignette. This replaces the death and the look of it; the look test, the fill and
the Hollow slowing while it is looked at stand.
**Research:** [Building dread toward a late reveal in horror games](https://csarko.sh/research/building-dread-toward-a-late-reveal),
§7 and §8.

## 0. What this is

Looking at a Hollow costs a player their sight and their hearing, never their life. The dark
closes in from the Hollow's side of the view and swells on a heartbeat; the woods go muffled
under the player's own heart; whispers start, circling the head. Looking away lets it go, more
slowly than it came. It is each player's own: a teammate looking elsewhere sees and hears none
of it.

The reasons are the research's. A game that punishes the look with death teaches players never
to look, and the watcher is on the climb to be looked at. The studio behind Amnesia took the
fail state out of its sanity system and found players more frightened without it. What a look
costs here is what the player needs most in the chase: a clear view and the sound of what is
behind them.

## 1. The rule (`sim/hollow.ts`)

- The look test is unchanged: a Hollow within 20° of the aim, within 120 m, with a clear line.
- `stare` fills from 0 to 1 over `HOLLOW_STARE_FILL_S` (6 s) of looking and empties over
  `HOLLOW_STARE_EMPTY_S` (8 s, was 3) of not: a look lingers.
- A full stare kills nobody. Contact is the only death.
- A Hollow that is looked at still walks at `HOLLOW_LOOK_FACTOR` of its speed, so a glance back in
  the chase buys distance and costs the screen and the ears while the player runs.
- The stare rides the snapshot as its byte, as before, and is in the fingerprint.

## 2. The lens (`game/stareLens.ts`)

A small state stepped once a frame on each screen from the local player's stare; the dead have
none. Nothing of it is on the wire.

- **Level.** The stare, eased over 0.12 s so the snapshot's steps do not show.
- **The heartbeat.** A phase that runs at 62 beats a minute as a stare begins and 84 at a full
  one, and a count of beats begun. A beat is two swells, the second 40 % of the first and 0.28 of
  a beat after it, each rising in 4.5 % of a beat and falling away; the last half of a beat is
  rest. The top rate is held under 90 on purpose: each beat darkens the frame's edge twice, and
  three such changes a second is the most a screen may flash (WCAG 2.3.1).
- **The side.** Where the Hollow nearest the aim stands on the screen, eased over 0.7 s, and held
  when none is in front. At the edge of the stare's cone it is at full length; dead ahead it is
  nothing, and the dark closes evenly.
- **The reach.** How far the dark has closed: the level through a smoothstep, so a glance is
  slight and the last seconds are steep, less up to 24 % between beats.

## 3. The picture

**The post path** (`shaders/grade.fragment.fx`, the last thing before the sRGB encode, over the
halation). One uniform, `stareShade`: the open centre's offset, the reach and a clock. The centre
is moved off the Hollow by up to 0.34 of the frame's half-width, so the dark reaches the Hollow
first. The edge of the dark is pushed in and out round the frame by three slow waves of the angle
about that centre (two, three and seven lobes, each turning at its own rate), so it is never a
ring. At a full stare an uneven hole about a quarter of the frame across is left, and the rest is
3 % of itself. At reach 0 the term is exactly nothing.

**The whole frame** also loses up to 30 % of its exposure (`STARE_DIM`), steadily, with no pulse
in it.

**The material path** (the low tier, no post passes) draws the same with Babylon's own vignette:
its weight raised by the reach and its centre moved the same way. It has no crawling edge.

## 4. The sound (`game/stareAudio.ts`, `game/ambientAudio.ts`)

All synthesized on the ambient context; no recording is shipped.

- **The world, muffled.** The rain, the wind, the drips and the wildlife are mixed through one bus
  and a low-pass that stands open at 20 kHz and shuts to 650 Hz at a full stare, by equal ratios,
  with the bus's level falling to 45 %.
- **The heart.** Each time the lens begins a beat the audio plays its two sounds, the second on
  the context's clock at the lens's own offset, so the ear and the eye keep one beat. A sound is a
  sine falling an octave from 62 Hz (74 for the second) under a 12 ms swell, with its octave above
  at a third of the level for speakers that cannot reach the first.
- **The whispers.** From a stare of 0.12, all there by 0.75. Four voices, built on the first
  stare. A voice is the shared noise through two band-passes set to a vowel's two resonances and
  opened for a syllable (220 to 550 ms with 140 to 400 between, since 2026-10-06; 80 to 260 as
  first built, and then with a hiss ahead of some, which read as a snare and is gone); at most
  two voices are in a phrase at once; syllables come in phrases of three to nine with a breath between, the breaths shorter
  as the stare deepens. Each voice sits on its own circle round the listener's head, 0.45 to
  0.9 m out, through a head-related panner. No voice says a word.

The heart and the whispers are not muffled: they are the player's own.

## 5. Tests

`test/sim/hollow.test.ts` (a full stare, held, kills nobody and ends nothing; the slower
emptying), `test/game/stareLens.test.ts` (the beat's shape and its rate under the flash limit,
the lens at rest, the first beat, the side, the shade), `test/game/gradeParams.test.ts` (the
record at rest is untouched, the dimming's floor, the shade it carries),
`test/game/post.test.ts` (the material path's vignette), `test/game/ambientAudio.test.ts` (the
world's bus and low-pass, the beat's two sounds in step, the voices' circles and their
syllables' envelopes, silence a second after the stare lets go).

## 6. Not in this

A sound for the Hollow itself, the birds cutting out at a sighting, the reveal at the crest, and
anything on the trail to find. The stare's levels are set by measurement, not by ear.
